A native app to watch and control an <b>Elegoo Centauri Carbon</b> 3D printer from your phone. No cloud, no account, no WebView: it talks to the printer directly over your local network, using the same protocol (SDCP) as the printer's own web interface.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Live status: progress, finish time, layer, temperatures, the current print's thumbnail</li>
<li>Camera: live view, fullscreen, chamber light toggle</li>
<li>Pause, resume and stop, with confirmation</li>
<li>Files: browse the printer's storage, start a print (plate side, auto-leveling, timelapse), delete files</li>
<li>History: past prints with thumbnails and outcome; print again or remove entries</li>
<li>Printer settings: rename, presets, nozzle and bed targets, speed, fans, homing</li>
<li>Notifications: progress while printing, and an alert when a print finishes, pauses, stops or fails, with the reason when the printer reports one (filament ran out, jam, print came loose...)</li>
<li>Demo mode: try every screen with a simulated printer</li>
<li>Tablet and foldable layouts; Material You colours; English, Dutch and Greek</li>
</ul>

Works with the original Centauri Carbon. The Centauri Carbon 2 uses a different protocol and isn't supported yet; other printers haven't been tried.

Unofficial app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Elegoo.
